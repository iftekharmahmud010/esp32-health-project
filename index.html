ESP32 Firmware & Hardware Wiring Guide

Supports SSD1306 Mini OLED, MAX30102 Oximeter, AD8232 ECG Sensor, and TMP36 Temperature Sensor.

1. Mini OLED (SSD1306 I2C)

VCC → 3.3V | GND → GND

SDA → GPIO 21 | SCL → GPIO 22

2. MAX30102 Oximeter (I2C)

VIN → 3.3V | GND → GND

SDA → GPIO 21 | SCL → GPIO 22

3. AD8232 ECG Sensor

3.3V → 3.3V | GND → GND

OUTPUT → GPIO 34 | LO+ → 32 | LO- → 33

4. TMP36 Temp Sensor

+Vs → 3.3V | GND → GND

Vout (Middle Pin) → GPIO 35
